# Multilingual

Arabic and English are first-class.

Separate:
master story, localized dialogue, voice timing, captions, typography and safe zones.

Arabic:
RTL-aware layout, Arabic typography, punctuation and line breaking.

English:
LTR layout and English typography.

Never assume a translation has identical timing.
Recalculate scene timing when required.
