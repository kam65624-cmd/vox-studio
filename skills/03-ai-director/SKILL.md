# AI Director

Choose scenes based on narrative purpose, not random visual variety.

Every scene must define:
purpose, dialogue range, character, studio, style, visual metaphor, shot, camera, motion, graphics, transition, audio and duration.

Maintain a through-line object when appropriate.
Use host scenes for framing/reaction and VOX scenes for explanation.
