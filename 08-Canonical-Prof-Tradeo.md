# Canonical Asset — Prof. Tradeo

## Identity
Prof. Tradeo is a smart, sarcastic, curious market-analysis host who loves analysis and coffee and learns from mistakes.

## Visual
Felt/puppet-like editorial character.
White wild hair, large bushy eyebrows and moustache, expressive round eyes, orange/red nose.
Wardrobe: dark green/teal blazer, mustard/caramel vest, white shirt, red bow tie, pocket square, chain/watch detail.

## Expressions
Happy, surprised, thinking, sarcastic, angry, shocked, hesitant, confident.

## Studio
Warm dark editorial study/podcast set:
wood desk, black microphone, black mug, notebook, papers, pen, books, warm desk lamp, shelves, bronze bull, globe/decor, pinned charts, world map, question mark paper, red-string board, plant and chart monitor.

## Set elements
microphone, coffee mug, book, analysis papers, lamp, bull symbol, books/props, chart screen, plant.

## VOX style

paper grain, halftone, ink edges, tape/stickers, rough tears, paper folds, charts, arrows, circles, archival imagery and controlled accent colors.
